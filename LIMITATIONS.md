# Limitations

This repository is a computational research record. There is no validated enhancement target, treatment, IQ improvement or demonstration that changing adult gene activity improves cognition.

1. **Phenotypes differ.** General cognition, reaction time, trail-making, nonverbal IQ, arithmetic items, mathematical self-report and education measure different constructs. Education and Gq remain labelled proxies. The exceptional-IQ cohort is a selected published sample, not a collection of identified exceptional people recruited here.
2. **Population transfer is limited.** Cognition GWAS and LD references are predominantly European. Molecular resources include different ancestries and clinical samples, but this does not establish cross-ancestry causal portability or support comparisons between population groups.
3. **Samples overlap.** UKB, COGENT, HRS and BATS recur. Within-family estimates address indirect/familial confounding but reuse some discovery participants. Single-cell, bulk and database annotations reuse donors, GWAS signals and models. Counting sources is not counting independent experiments.
4. **Association is not an intervention effect.** Inherited effects may act during development, through multiple tissues or through indirect pathways. Colocalisation is conditional on priors, LD and causal-variant assumptions; it does not demonstrate mediation or a safe molecular direction.
5. **Candidate genes remain contested.** Positional L2G, PoPS features, transcript QTLs and gene-level colocalisation have different evidentiary meaning. Neither high PIP nor rare loss of function proves benefit from increasing activity. The high-confidence and adult shortlists are empty.
6. **Historical defects are material.** Gene-universe loss, unpropagated GO, lineage duplication, unsigned tail comparisons, KDE-Tweedie instability and AlphaGenome sign aggregation are documented. Some corrections followed observation of results, so protocol-freeze language must not imply a fully prospective independent analysis.
7. **Power and multiple testing matter.** Weak replication samples, scarce rare-variant carriers and nominal set-level triggers make nulls and apparent positives fragile. Missing or inaccessible data are untestable, not evidence of no effect. Gene ranks have wide intervals and are not calibrated causal probabilities.
8. **Molecular context is incomplete.** Bulk mixture, diseased/aged donors, postmortem tissue, cellular states and developmental windows limit generalisation. Significant-only brainSCOPE files cannot support unbiased new colocalisation. Its shared donors cannot rescue SH2B1 independence.
9. **Regulatory model correction is incomplete end to end.** The sign code is fixed and synthetic cases pass; raw AlphaGenome predictions are absent from Git. Historical directions/agreement fields are unresolved and excluded from current voting. A gene-only join to observed QTLs also requires locus/variant/allele-aware validation before any direction is reinstated.
10. **Reproducibility has boundaries.** The source tree preserves code, rules, seeds, summaries and acquisition hashes. External MAGMA/LD resources, R environments, large inputs and restricted data are not all bundled or fully locked. A passing test suite is not an independent reproduction of the science.
11. **AI assistance requires scrutiny.** Historical AI agents performed coding, curation and interpretation. Automated consistency tests and this release audit cannot replace expert review of every annotation or experimental validation. The project has not been peer reviewed.
12. **Rights are heterogeneous.** MIT applies to project-authored code. BrainSpan derivatives retain noncommercial terms; other upstream sources impose attribution/access conditions. Public availability does not automatically permit relicensing. See [data rights](DATA_LICENSE.md).

The appropriate next scientific step is independent causal-gene adjudication with better molecular and within-family evidence, under the existing stopping rules. This release supplies no experimental intervention design.
