## Optional thread

1. Can human cognitive genetics point to biological mechanisms that remain modifiable after neurons mature? Hamiltonian Labs is opening the code and evidence record behind a computational investigation: https://github.com/MoheetBrain/humanintelligence

2. The earlier screen assessed 18,418 genes; 375 Bonferroni-significant genes fell in 159 LD-defined loci. In a within-family comparison, 109 of 146 testable lead alleles were concordant. These are different analytical stages, not counts of causal genes.

3. Later analysis combines fine-mapping, brain eQTL colocalisation, developmental context, cross-trait statistics and dependency-aware gene assessment. DNPH1, TTBK1 and FAM131A remain moderate hypotheses; no gene reaches high confidence or the strict adult-candidate gate.

4. The limits matter: overlapping cohorts, mostly European cognition GWAS, uncertain gene attribution and no validated intervention. We also found and excluded faulty AlphaGenome directional aggregation. The correction and negative findings are public.

5. AI assisted programming and research; it does not validate the biology. We welcome independent checks of allele alignment, cohort independence, competing genes and reproducibility. Original data terms and access boundaries are documented. No treatment or IQ increase is claimed.
